Refunds & Cancellations

Refund policy

Last updated: October 2026. Applies to paid CYTHON CTF 1.0 team entry passes.

1. Cancellation by you

Cancel at least 48 hours before the event start (30 Oct 2026, 10:00 AM IST) for a full refund to the original payment method. Cancellations within 48 hours of the start are not refundable.

2. Cancellation by the organiser

If NxtGenSec cancels the event, all paid teams receive a full refund within 7–10 business days.

3. Disqualification

Teams disqualified for rule violations (cheating, attacking infrastructure, flag sharing) are not eligible for refunds.

4. How to request

Email demonxtgensec@gmail.com with your team name, registered email, and payment reference ID. Refunds go only to the original payment method.
