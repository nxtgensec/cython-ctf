About the Event

What is CYTHON CTF?

CYTHON CTF is a cybersecurity Capture The Flag competition designed to bring curious minds, students, developers, and security enthusiasts together through practical security challenges.

Participants work through problems, analyze evidence, uncover weaknesses in intentionally designed challenges, and submit flags according to the competition rules.

The event encourages technical curiosity, ethical security practices, problem-solving, and continuous learning.

Rules & Eligibility

Play Smart. Play Fair.

General Rules

  • 01Follow the official competition instructions.
  • 02Work only on targets explicitly authorized by the event.
  • 03Do not attack the website, infrastructure, or other participants.
  • 04Do not interfere with event availability or challenge integrity.
  • 05Do not share flags or solutions in violation of the official event rules.
  • 06Do not impersonate other participants.
  • 07Report suspected vulnerabilities in the event infrastructure responsibly.
  • 08Respect the event's final eligibility, team-size, and submission requirements.

Eligibility

  • Open to students, professionals, and other interested participants.
  • Individual or team participation, subject to the configured policy.
  • Participants must provide accurate information.
  • Additional requirements, if any, must be published before registration.
